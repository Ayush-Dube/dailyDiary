```
🌳 AIRCRAFT THRUST — DOUBTS → SOLUTIONS → KEY POINTS

1️⃣ THRUST
   └─ Thrust = force produced by accelerating air/gas
      → Air one way ⇒ machine gets reaction force opposite way

2️⃣ 🚁 HELICOPTER
   ├─ Rotor blades = rotating wings
   ├─ Blades accelerate air DOWNWARD
   ├─ Reaction force = UPWARD rotor thrust
   ├─ Thrust = Weight → helicopter HOVERS
   ├─ Thrust > Weight → helicopter RISES
   └─ Thrust < Weight → helicopter DESCENDS

   ❓ Doubt:
      "Agar thrust hai toh helicopter continuously kyu nahi rise karta?"

   ✅ Solution:
      Hover mein UPWARD THRUST = DOWNWARD WEIGHT

3️⃣ 🌀 HELICOPTER BLADE PITCH
   ├─ Pitch = blade ka angle
   ├─ Low pitch  → blade relatively flat → less thrust
   └─ High pitch → blade more angled → generally more thrust

   🎛️ Collective
      → sab blades ka pitch together change
      → controls HOW MUCH thrust

   🎛️ Cyclic
      → blade pitch rotation ke different positions par change
      → rotor disc tilts
      → thrust direction changes
      → helicopter forward/back/side move karta hai

4️⃣ 🏠 TABLE FAN

   ❓ Doubt:
      "Fan ghoomta hai, toh table se udta kyu nahi?"

   ✅ Solution:
      Fan air ko mainly HORIZONTAL direction mein accelerate
      karta hai.

      Table fan ko physically support karta hai.

   💡 Important:
      Fan mein bhi reaction force/thrust hoti hai,
      but it is not designed to lift itself.

5️⃣ ✈️ PROPELLER

   ├─ Propeller = rotating aerodynamic blades
   ├─ Air → BACKWARD
   └─ Reaction → FORWARD THRUST

   ❓ Doubt:
      "Kya propeller blade ka angle change hota hai?"

   ✅ Some propellers = FIXED PITCH
      Others = VARIABLE / CONSTANT-SPEED PITCH

      LOW PITCH  → blade relatively flat
      HIGH PITCH → blade more steep/angled

6️⃣ 🔥 JET ENGINES

   🌱 Basic evolution:

      TURBOJET ─────────→ TURBOFAN
       (earlier)             (later development)


   A) TURBOJET

      Air
       ↓
      Compressor
       ↓
      Combustor
       ↓
      Turbine
       ↓
      FAST EXHAUST →→→→
                       ↓
                 forward thrust

      ❌ No large front fan
      ✅ Main thrust = high-speed exhaust


   B) TURBOFAN

      Air → BIG FAN ─────────→ Bypass air
                │
                └→ Core → Combustor → Turbine → Exhaust

      ✅ Large fan contributes major thrust
      ✅ Core exhaust also contributes thrust


   C) TURBOPROP

      Turbine engine
           ↓
         shaft
           ↓
      🌀 PROPELLER
           ↓
      air backward
           ↓
      forward thrust

      ✅ Similar basic aerodynamic idea to helicopter rotor
      ❌ But propeller thrust is mainly FORWARD
      🚁 Helicopter rotor thrust is mainly UPWARD

7️⃣ 🔑 BIGGEST KEY IDEA

   ✈️ Propeller
      air → BACKWARD
      aircraft → FORWARD

   🚁 Helicopter rotor
      air → DOWNWARD
      helicopter → UPWARD

   🔥 Jet engine
      gas → BACKWARD
      aircraft → FORWARD


   🧠 SAME FUNDAMENTAL PHYSICS:

      Air/gas ko accelerate karo
              ↓
      Momentum change
              ↓
      Opposite reaction force
              ↓
           THRUST 🚀


8️⃣ ⚠️ IMPORTANT CORRECTION

   ❌ "Helicopter blade air ko sirf suction se kheenchta hai"

   ✅ Better understanding:

      Rotor blade = aerodynamic wing
      + blade pitch
      + rotation
            ↓
      aerodynamic force
            ↓
      air gets DOWNWARD momentum
            ↓
      DOWNWASH
            ↓
      rotor gets UPWARD reaction force


9️⃣ 🎯 ONE-LINE MEMORY

   🌀 ROTATING BLADE ≈ ROTATING WING

   Change blade angle / pitch
             ↓
   Change aerodynamic force
             ↓
   Change thrust
             ↓
   Control the aircraft 🚁✈️
```
